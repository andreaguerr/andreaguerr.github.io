## Current position

I am a Lecturer and Royal Society University Research Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at **City St George’s, University of London**, where I joined in January 2025.

My research focuses on the S-matrix bootstrap and its applications to strong interactions, quantum gravity and non-perturbative quantum field theory.

## Background

Before joining City St George’s, I held research positions at CERN, the Perimeter Institute, Tel Aviv University, ICTP-SAIFR and Chulalongkorn University. I completed my PhD at the University of Rome Tor Vergata.

For a detailed employment history, see my [university profile](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/people/academics/andrea-guerrieri).
