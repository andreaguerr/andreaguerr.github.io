## Research directions

### S-matrix bootstrap

What can we learn about a quantum theory from the consistency of its scattering amplitudes? I combine analyticity, crossing symmetry, unitarity and dispersion relations with numerical optimisation to bound interactions and cross-sections, including in regimes where perturbation theory is unreliable.

### QCD and hadrons

I apply these methods to the strong interactions, connecting scattering data with fundamental constraints. My work includes pion amplitudes, hadronic resonances and glueball couplings, with both phenomenological and large-$N$ perspectives.

### Quantum gravity

I study how scattering consistency constrains gravitational effective field theories and their higher-derivative interactions. A central question is where string theory and M-theory sit within the space of amplitudes allowed by unitarity, analyticity and crossing symmetry.

### Matrix models

I also work on matrix quantum mechanics and large-$N$ systems, using bootstrap techniques and numerical methods to investigate non-perturbative physics. These models offer a setting in which analytic constraints and numerical calculations can inform one another.

## Selected papers

### Cross-Section Bootstrap: Unveiling the Froissart Amplitude

Miguel Correia, Alessandro Georgoudis and Andrea L. Guerrieri<br>
**arXiv:2506.04313 · 2025 · Preprint**

We derive a universal bound on the integrated total scattering cross-section at finite energies, expressed through a low-energy coefficient constrained by the S-matrix bootstrap. We also investigate candidate extremal amplitudes with rising cross-sections, diffraction patterns and Regge-like resonance spectra.

[Read on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04313) · [INSPIRE record](https://inspirehep.net/literature/2929755)

### From data to the analytic S-matrix: A Bootstrap fit of the pion scattering amplitude

Andrea Guerrieri, Kelian Häring and Ning Su<br>
**SciPost Physics 20(2), 034 · 2026**

We combine scattering data with analyticity, crossing symmetry and unitarity to reconstruct pion–pion amplitudes. The bootstrap fit reproduces low-energy chiral predictions and known resonances, and predicts a doubly charged tetraquark candidate near 2 GeV.

[Read on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23333) · [Published article](https://doi.org/10.21468/SciPostPhys.20.2.034)

### Where is M-theory in the space of scattering amplitudes?

Andrea Guerrieri, Harish Murali, Joao Penedones and Pedro Vieira<br>
**Journal of High Energy Physics 06 (2023), 064**

We use the S-matrix bootstrap to constrain the leading corrections to maximal supergravity in nine, ten and eleven dimensions. The resulting numerical lower bounds lie close to, but do not coincide with, the smallest values realised by string theory and M-theory.

[Read on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00151) · [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2023)064)
