## How much of QCD can be reconstructed from scattering?

Analyticity, crossing symmetry and unitarity place strong restrictions on amplitudes. I am interested in how these constraints, together with experimental input and large-*N* methods, can reveal hadron spectra and interactions beyond perturbation theory.

## What controls the growth of total cross-sections?

The Froissart bound constrains asymptotic growth, but experiments probe finite energies. Understanding useful finite-energy bounds — and the amplitudes approaching them — connects bootstrap methods to diffraction, resonances and high-energy hadronic scattering.

## How restrictive is consistency in quantum gravity?

Graviton scattering provides a way to constrain higher-derivative corrections to supergravity, such as <i>R</i><sup>4</sup>, <i>D</i><sup>4</sup><i>R</i><sup>4</sup> and beyond. A central question is how inelasticity changes the allowed space and its relation to string theory and M-theory.

## What can the bootstrap teach us about matrix models?

Matrix quantum mechanics and large-*N* models provide settings in which to develop non-perturbative methods. I am interested in the interplay between bootstrap constraints, analytic limits and numerical calculations.
