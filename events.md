## 2026

### Bootstrap 2026

**London, United Kingdom · 3–14 August 2026**<br>
*Co-organiser, with Shai Chester, Petr Kravchuk and Andreas Stergiou*

A two-week conference bringing together researchers using bootstrap methods to study quantum field theory and quantum gravity. Topics included the conformal bootstrap, the S-matrix bootstrap and matrix models.

[Conference archive](https://indico.global/event/16114/)

### From First Principles to Future Colliders

**Amplitudes, Bootstraps and Energy Correlators**<br>
**Aspen Center for Physics · 12 July–2 August 2026**<br>
*Co-organiser, with Nima Arkani-Hamed, Cari Cesarotti and Ian Moult*

A three-week summer programme connecting scattering amplitudes, bootstrap methods, lattice QCD and collider physics, with a focus on first-principles approaches to particle collisions.

[Programme archive](https://aspenphys.org/event/from-first-principles-to-future-colliders-amplitudes-bootstraps-and-energy-correlators/)
